## What this means for step 2 (the recipe)
The reference is built from ~20 scene families, but **4 carry half the video** (stage 17 %, space 13 %, stories 10 %,
science/rays 18 %). It always returns to the home stage between chapters (36 stage shots spread over all 15 minutes).
The recipe doc should teach the director: one home stage + one world per chapter + mini-stories for history + a device
per kind of sentence (number → count/pill, "how big" → measure/bar, "what next" → mystery, "who" → line-up, list → icon rows).
