## Why it works (in one line)
ONE question ("what is the most expensive thing that exists?") turned into a **game the viewer plays**: a leaderboard of
glowing podiums that keeps growing. Every chapter adds a contender, its price, and a "bigger than the last one" moment.

## The look
- **Dark stage, neon glow.** Deep navy/purple space with tiny stars; a **synthwave purple grid floor** with a glowing
  horizon line is the "home stage" (≈35 % of shots). 67 % of pixels are dark; colour comes from gold, neon green, magenta.
  Story scenes switch the palette: warm sunset (history/years), beige paper map (geography), night city (real estate),
  red (war), deep blue (science/space). → a palette per chapter, one home stage for the whole video.
- **Hero objects are rich, semi-realistic illustrated cut-outs** (diamond with sparkles, crown, Mona Lisa in gold frame,
  Buckingham Palace, isometric land tile, antimatter capsule, gloved hand, Earth, sun) on dark backgrounds with
  **sunburst rays + sparkles** behind them. Story characters are the opposite: **flat, faceless, round heads with one
  identifying hat** (beret painter, top-hat buyer, miner helmet, Napoleon bicorne, teepee people).
- **Text = Arabic heavy rounded font** (very bold, like Baloo Bhaijaan 2 ExtraBold). White with ONE key word in gold.
  Big numbers in gold with dark outline and glow. Labels are **pills**: gold pill = the hero/current value, white pill =
  previous, grey small pill = old/small. Year = black rounded tile with gold border + gold digits + small "سنة" pill.
- **The host** (the creator's own faceless character: black head, gold rim-light, white/black hoodie, gold accents) stands
  bottom-left (sometimes right), ~45 % of the frame height, points at the subject, shrugs, reacts. He is in the hook,
  every leaderboard beat, reactions and the ending — not in the mini-stories. *(We use AZTIKA in this role — never copy
  this character.)*

## The signature devices (steal the IDEA, build our own)
1. **The growing leaderboard** (1:34, 2:30, 4:04, 5:41, 6:22, 6:31, 7:42, 9:33, 12:16, 14:00): green glowing podiums on
   the grid floor, each object on top, price pill above. New contender → its podium grows taller than the others; old
   ones shrink/slide right. A dashed bracket + giant "×2" shows the jump. It is the video's spine and its recap.
2. **The mystery box**: a golden "?" block on a glowing pedestal = "what is next / unknown" (0:09, 0:28, 3:55, 12:16).
3. **Counting numbers**: weights/prices count up live (100 → 107 → 122 → … → 3,106 قيراط) — motion inside a held shot.
4. **Word-by-word headlines**: a short Arabic headline builds word by word with the speech (رحلة → رحلة بين …),
   placed top-right, key word in gold.
5. **Mini-story vignettes** for history: a 3–10 s staged scene with flat characters, a speech bubble with ✔/✖, a
   progress bar (89 %), a map with an arrow from Italy to France. Story first, number second.
6. **Scale comparisons**: map overlays (Louisiana inside the USA ×2), a 170 km line in the desert, budget bars
   (bars 'military spending' vs 'economy'), "1 g" sample vs trucks/boxes — always one familiar thing next to the huge one.
7. **Transitions**: diagonal gold/orange stripe wipes (0:09), white flash, zoom-through; stamps "تم البيع" / "مو الأغلى"
   slapped on with a shake.
8. **The ending**: everything in one recap leaderboard → "the universe's invoice" receipt totalling 0 $ → the thesis line
   over the galaxy → neon-sign sign-off «في أمان الله» with the host. A real emotional landing, not "thanks for watching".
