## The format
- **A permanent white header bar** (≈ 10 % of the height, thin black rule under it) shows the current chapter/topic in
  plain Arabic (الإمبراطورية الرومانية · روما في عزّها · طرق وقنوات وجيش …). It changes every 30–60 s → the viewer always
  knows where they are in the story, like a book's running head. Cheap, very clear, works for long lists ("6 empires").
- **Two picture modes, alternating:**
  1. **Painted worlds** — AI-generated full-frame backgrounds in one consistent cartoon style (thick dark outlines, warm
     flat colours, comic look): the Forum with the Colosseum, a red throne hall, Ctesiphon arch by the river, desert,
     Tenochtitlan on the lake, the Great Wall, a burning street. The SAME background is reused 3–6 times with different
     characters/labels on it (Rome forum: empty → crowd → soldiers → coins) — consistency and very low cost.
  2. **White explainer stage** — pure white with stick-figure people (round white heads, simple faces, one costume item:
     crown, Roman helmet, Aztec feathers, Mongol hat). Concepts are acted out: coin "silver → cheap metal" with an arrow,
     a rising inflation graph, a treasure chest next to a fire (= treasury burned), two Mongols pulling a crown apart.
- **A recurring narrator stick-man with a red scarf** asks the questions (thinking pose = "why?", thumbs-up = subscribe).
- **Maps** (beige land, blue sea, red empire) — the territory **spreads in red** over ~1 s; then a label pops (بريطانيا).
- **Text** = yellow (or red for bad news / green for victory) with a heavy black outline, meme-subtitle style, 2–6 words,
  placed next to what it names. Names are labels on characters (خالد بن الوليد, رستم, موكتيزوما).
- **Respectful quote cards**: cream card with gold border for Quran verses and historical quotes (3:39, 5:16).
- Bright: average brightness 169/255, 35 % pure white. Louder mix (≈ −25 dB).
- Subscribe ask at 0:33 (after the promise, before chapter 1).

## Weaknesses (do NOT copy)
- Almost no motion: motion ≈ 1 everywhere — still images with elements popping in; no camera moves, no parallax.
- Long holds of 10–16 s on one painted still with one text pop. Empty white frames (#38, #95, #118).
- Generated art occasionally inconsistent (characters change style between modes).
