# inner-voice · rules for every chapter (read with your chapter brief)
**Video:** "The Voice in Your Head Makes No Sound" — inner speech = *language with the sound taken out*. Plan: videos/inner-voice/plan.md.
**Visual language (keep it identical in every chapter):**
- SPOKEN / real sound = solid ink: solid speech bubbles, solid ink waveforms, solid lines between people.
- INNER voice = the same shapes in **dashed theory-plum** (kit `Bubble kind='inner'`, shared `SilentWave silent=1`).
- Inside a head = ink / plum mind-space (kit `MindSpace`, `HeadProfile`). Outside world = warm paper, modern settings.
- Oxblood = ONLY the one key beat of a shot. Green caption = confirmed finding · plum caption = model/theory/idea ·
  mustard caption = debated/limits/caution.
**Shared components** (`../shared/`, see docs/ASSETS.md for dials): `SilentWave`, `ThoughtContent` (words/image/feeling/
awareness/wordless), `Beeper` (+`beepFlash`), `DecoderRig` (person + implant + cable + screen). Use them — never redraw them.
**People:** generic stylised modern figures (casual/labcoat/suit), varied skin tones; real researchers appear only as
`NameTag`s (no likeness). The BCI participant is shown with dignity (calm, seated, no medical gore).
**Text:** only the strings in the brief (no subtitles). Tags ≥ 34 px, Kickers ≥ 30 px, body ≥ 40 px, headlines ≥ 64 px,
all inside SAFE (96 px) and in clear space — never over a busy shape. Ink on paper or paper on ink.
**Timing:** times in the briefs are sentence starts; get exact word times with
`node tools/cue.mjs --slug inner-voice "word"` (ALWAYS pass `--slug inner-voice`). Reveal 0.1–0.25 s before the stressed word.
⚠ C01 only: word times before 8.3 s are wrong (speech recognition missed the cold open) — use the times in the C01 brief.
**Motion:** brisk; new visual info every 2–4 s; nothing static > 4 s (breathe, particles, blink); eased camera moves;
hook-level shots are layered scenes with depth and light, never a lone object on a flat backdrop.
**Owner preferences:** no nudity in anything; contact sheets, not live preview.
