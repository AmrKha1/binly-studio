# plan — «أغلى غلطة في التاريخ» (ar-costly-mistakes · 12:12 · AZTIKA night look)

**Logline:** we climb a staircase of human mistakes, each pricier than the last — a missing pen stroke, a hard drive in
the bin, two pizzas, a wrong bank transfer, a fake job interview, two "no"s — to a summit where the most expensive
mistake carries the SMALLEST number: 4 million pounds and a signature that cost Egypt 81 years.

**The question:** what is the most expensive mistake a human ever made? **Open loops:** (1) the summit «؟» from the hook —
"the most expensive one has the smallest number, in an Arab land, paid by people not yet born" (paid off in C10–C11);
(2) the pizza aside "the real bill arrives years later — we'll come back to it" (paid off in C11 thesis).

**Game device = StairGame (the spine).** Stairs rise RIGHT → LEFT; the summit holds a gold «؟» from the hook. Every
mistake takes its step with its object + its price pill exactly as spoken. The rule changes twice and the board SHOWS
it: Chernobyl (700 مليار after 4 تريليون — "measured in people", a red step) and Suez (4 مليون takes the SUMMIT — gold
flash, «الأغلى»). Recap at the end lists the steps in the order spoken.

**Motif (this video's identity) — THE BILL (الفاتورة):** every chapter ends with a paper bill that prints "then" vs
"today" and flies onto its step; for Suez the bill does not go to the man who signed — it travels down a timeline to
people born generations later. Second motif — **THE DOOR (الباب):** the Binance file "was the door"; the Suez sale "was
the first door that opened" (same glowing door piece, two meanings).

**Visual thesis:** a night game-show staircase where every step is a small disaster told like a story; the price is
always shown as a moment you can see (a stroke, a bag, a slice, a button press, a download bar, a handshake, a pen).
